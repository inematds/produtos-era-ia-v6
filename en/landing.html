Who it's for

It's for you if

  • You have an idea for a digital product or service and want to know if it's worth the effort.
  • You already use an AI chat, even if only for simple questions.
  • You have little time and no technical background.
  • You prefer to learn by doing.

It's not for you if

  • You want to learn to program or build the product step by step.
  • You already have a large product with a marketing team.
  • You want traditional video lessons.
